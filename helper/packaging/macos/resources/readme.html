What gets installed

  • The helper: ~/Library/Application Support/VaultAgent/vaultagent
  • A login item that starts it: ~/Library/LaunchAgents/dev.bme.vaultagent.plist
  • An errors-only log: ~/Library/Logs/VaultAgent.log (never your notes or prompts)

Chats are kept in ~/.bme-agent-sessions, one folder per vault.

Your agents stay as they are

VaultAgent never changes the settings of Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode. Every setting it applies is passed to that one run and nothing else.

Removing it

In the editor's agent panel, open the ⋯ menu and choose Uninstall VaultAgent…. The helper, its login item and its log are removed. Your chats are kept.

“Background Items Added”

macOS will tell you a background item was added. That is VaultAgent's login item.
